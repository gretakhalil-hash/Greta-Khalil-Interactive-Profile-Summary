About

Where the signed deal meets the service behind it.

Over the past years, I have delivered significant achievements in service design and strategic account management.

I craft integrated service designs for complex deals, blending people, processes, and technology.

As recent accomplishments, I successfully secured the Airtel Africa MS renewal and new scope deal, contributed to the Orange Business win in the Europe region, and participated in E2E package reviews and repository updates to drive quality assurance and process optimization across the contract management framework.

I was recognized for leadership and collaboration through my nomination as Co-Chair of the WE LEaD community at CSG, where I co-organized the 2025 Global Symposium in South Africa as a member of the planning committee (15 speakers, 80 in-person and 150 virtual attendees).

I also actively mentor new colleagues within the Service Design team and further developed my leadership and talent development capabilities through the BEBS Leadership and Talent Management program.
